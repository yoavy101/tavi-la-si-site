Delete your Tavi La Si account

Tavi La Si — ear training app, published by Yoav Trabelsi

You can request the deletion of your Tavi La Si account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion requests are verified and completed within 30 days.

Option 1 — from inside the app

Open Settings → Delete my account. This opens a pre-filled deletion-request email; just send it from the email address you registered with.

Option 2 — without the app

Email us the request directly (no need to have the app installed):

Request account deletion by email

Or write to yoavtrabelsi1@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Account deletion request".

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and login credentials)
  • Your profile: display name, avatar, friend invite code
  • All gameplay data: scores, XP, streaks, exercise history
  • Your friend connections and leaderboard entries

We may retain minimal records where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, and only for as long as necessary for that purpose. Data stored only on your device (settings, avatar items, guest progress) is removed by uninstalling the app.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
