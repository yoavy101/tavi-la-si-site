Privacy Policy — Tavi La Si

Effective date: July 25, 2026

1. Introduction & who we are

Tavi La Si (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is an ear-training app for musicians, published on Google Play by Yoav Trabelsi (the “developer”), the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

We built Tavi La Si to collect as little data as possible. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data. This policy explains exactly what we do collect, why, and what your choices are.

Contact: yoavtrabelsi1@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account data (only if you create an account)

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Email address and password — used to create and secure your account. Authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase; your password is stored only in hashed form and we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Display name — the name you choose; shown to other users (see below).
  • Chosen animal avatar — your in-app character.

2.2 Gameplay data (only if you have an account)

While you play signed in, we store on our backend:

  • Exercise results: the exercise mode, your score, number of correct answers out of total, and XP earned.
  • Your total XP, daily login streak, and last login date.
  • A randomly generated friend invite code, and the list of friend connections you make by exchanging codes.
  • Your account creation date.

Visible to other users: your display name, animal avatar, and XP appear on the in-app leaderboards (global top players and your friends list) and are visible to users you connect with. Your email address is never shown to other users.

2.3 Diagnostic logs — stored on your device only

The app keeps a small technical log on your device (the last 12 app sessions) to help diagnose crashes and bugs. It records timestamps, app events, error messages and crash traces, plus your device model, operating system version, and app version.

These logs never leave your device automatically. They are stored in the app’s private storage and are shared only if you choose to: opening Settings → Report a problem and tapping Share hands the log text to the Android share sheet, where you pick the recipient (for example, emailing it to us). If you never tap Share, we never receive any log. Uninstalling the app deletes the logs.

2.4 Guest mode — nothing leaves your device

You can use the app without an account by choosing Play as guest. In guest mode, no personal data is sent to our servers at all: your guest profile, progress, and settings exist only in the app’s local storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app (guest progress is not synced or backed up).

2.5 Other data stored locally on your device only

The following stays in the app’s local storage and is never transmitted: your app settings (theme, instrument, note range, playback speed, etc.), avatar-shop purchases and equipped items, practice-reminder schedules, and a cached copy of your own profile for offline use.

2.6 Practice reminders (notifications)

If you enable practice reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device after asking for the Android notification permission. No push-notification service is used and no notification data is sent to any server. You can disable reminders in the app or in Android settings at any time.

2.7 Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect: precise or approximate location; contacts; photos, videos, or files; microphone or audio recordings (the app plays sound but never records); advertising identifiers; browsing history; financial or payment information (the in-app “Notes ♪” currency is earned by playing — there are no real-money purchases); or any special categories of personal data.

Like virtually all internet services, our backend provider’s servers process your IP address transiently when the app communicates with them (for connection handling and security); we do not use it to track you.

3. How we use your data

We use the data above only to:

  • Create, secure, and operate your account (authentication, session management).
  • Save and display your progress: scores, XP, streaks, and exercise history.
  • Operate social features you choose to use: leaderboards and friend connections via invite codes.
  • Award daily login bonuses based on your streak.
  • Diagnose problems — only when you voluntarily share a diagnostic log with us.
  • Respond to your messages and support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not combine it with data from other sources.

4. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process your data on these bases:

ProcessingLegal basis
Account creation, authentication, saving progress, leaderboards, friends Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the app features you signed up for
Security, abuse prevention, and reviewing diagnostic logs you send us Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the service secure and working; you may object at any time
Practice-reminder notifications, and any log you choose to share Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing
Responding to legal requests, if ever required Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

Providing an email address and password is required only if you want an account; without them you can still use the app fully in guest mode. Where Brazil’s LGPD applies, we rely on the equivalent legal bases in LGPD Article 7 (contract execution, legitimate interest, consent, legal obligation).

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. There are no ad networks or analytics providers in the app. In the terms used by U.S. state privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act): we do not “sell” personal information and do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months.

Your data is disclosed only to:

  • Supabase, Inc. — our backend hosting provider (data processor). Supabase hosts our database and authentication service on Amazon Web Services infrastructure and processes your account and gameplay data solely on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. Supabase uses infrastructure subprocessors (such as AWS) to provide this service.
  • Other users — limited to your display name, animal avatar, and XP, shown on leaderboards and to friends as described in section 2.2.
  • Authorities, only if we are legally required to do so (we have never received such a request).

Google Play (Google LLC) distributes the app and handles the download/install process under its own privacy policy, as an independent controller — we receive no personal data from Google about you.

6. Data retention & deletion

  • Account and gameplay data are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Account deletion: you can request deletion of your account and all associated data (profile, scores, XP, friend connections) at any time by emailing yoavtrabelsi1@gmail.com from the address you registered with. We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days. You can also request deletion from inside the app (Settings → Delete my account, which opens a pre-filled request email). We may retain minimal records where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, and only for as long as necessary for that purpose.
  • Diagnostic logs live only on your device: the app keeps at most the last 12 sessions and deletes older ones automatically; uninstalling the app removes them all. If you email us a log, we delete it once the issue is resolved.
  • Local data (guest progress, settings, avatar items, reminder schedules) is deleted when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, Brazil’s LGPD, and Canada’s PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access — ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and information about how it is processed.
  • Rectification / correction — have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure / deletion — have your account and data deleted (see section 6).
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction of processing — ask us to limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, for processing based on consent, without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing — not applicable in practice, because we do not sell or share personal data for advertising; we honor this right by design.
  • Non-discrimination — we will never treat you differently for exercising any privacy right.

To exercise any right, email yoavtrabelsi1@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days (or sooner where local law requires) and extend these rights to all users regardless of where they live.

Complaints: you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority — in the EU, your national supervisory authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO); in Brazil, the ANPD; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC). We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

8. International transfers

Our backend is hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in Switzerland (AWS region eu-central-2, Zurich), a country recognized by the European Commission as providing adequate data protection. If you use the app from another country, your data will be transferred to and stored in that region. Where personal data of EEA, UK, or Swiss users is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer is protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), incorporated into our data processing agreement with Supabase.

9. Security

  • All communication between the app and our backend uses TLS encryption (HTTPS); the app makes no unencrypted network connections.
  • Data at rest on Supabase is encrypted with AES-256; Supabase maintains SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 certifications.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by Supabase Auth.
  • Database access rules restrict what each signed-in user can read and write.
  • Diagnostic logs are stored in app-private storage that other apps cannot read.

No system is perfectly secure, but the small amount of data we hold and the absence of third-party SDKs keep the attack surface minimal. If a data breach affecting you ever occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

10. Children’s privacy

Tavi La Si is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the minimum age required for consent to data processing in your country (up to 16 in some EU member states). If you are under that age, please use guest mode (which sends no data to us) or ask a parent or guardian to manage an account. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us at yoavtrabelsi1@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
